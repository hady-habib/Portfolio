Senior Electrical Construction Engineer (8–12 Years)

Location: Saudi Arabia | Project: House of Al Saud Museum | Employer: Sharqawi Co.

Role Overview

Sharqawi Co. is seeking a Senior Electrical Construction Engineer to lead electrical execution for the House of Al Saud Museum Project. This role requires strong technical depth, proven site leadership, and the ability to coordinate complex MEP interfaces in a fast‑paced, high‑standards environment.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead electrical discipline execution from shop drawings to testing & commissioning.
  • Review and approve shop drawings, material submittals, method statements, and RFIs.
  • Oversee installation quality for power, lighting, grounding, cable routing, panels, and equipment.
  • Coordinate with mechanical, civil, and low‑current teams to resolve clashes and maintain progress.
  • Manage consultant inspections and ensure documentation is audit‑ready.
  • Identify risks early and drive corrective actions to protect schedule and quality.
  • Mentor junior engineers and supervise site teams.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering.
  • 8–12 years of experience in building construction (KSA preferred).
  • Strong knowledge of electrical systems, codes, and MEP coordination.
  • Proven leadership in complex or high‑value projects.
  • Excellent communication and documentation skills.
  • Transferable Iqama preferred.
