About

Senior Construction Manager (Mechanical/MEP) with 15+ years of experience leading construction delivery of Mechanical, Plumbing and MEP packages on flagship projects in Saudi Arabia. Career portfolio of approximately SAR 1.3B+ across stadium, royal, cultural and commercial developments, including SAR 1.1B+ under my direct management.

I manage the full construction lifecycle, from constructability review and tender handover through execution, testing and commissioning, handover and closeout.
